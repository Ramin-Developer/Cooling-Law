\begin{abstract}
This report presents a compact introduction to Newton's law of cooling and its
numerical treatment in this repository. The intended audience is undergraduate
students with a background in elementary calculus and introductory differential
equations. We summarize the mathematical model, describe the implemented
discretization, and explain how correctness and reproducibility are verified.
\end{abstract}
